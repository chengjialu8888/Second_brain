# Wiki Lint

> A maintenance pass that finds gaps, contradictions, duplicate entities, missing citations, stale pages, and useful follow-up questions.

## Definition

Wiki Lint is the active quality loop for a Second Brain. It keeps the brain from becoming a passive archive.

## Why It Matters

The differentiator is not just that memory can be queried, but that it can ask back.

## My Current Read

The MVP lint should be simple: broken wikilinks, duplicate titles, missing frontmatter, missing Timeline, and unresolved open questions.

## Counterexamples / Risks

Automatic semantic rewrites are risky. Lint should produce questions and repair plans before changing meaning.
