# Compiled Truth

> The current synthesized view of an entity, separated from its append-only evidence timeline.

## Definition

Compiled Truth is everything above the `---` divider in an entity page. Timeline is everything below it.

## Why It Matters

Users need a fast current answer and a way to trace the evidence. Combining both in one undifferentiated note makes pages harder for agents to maintain.

## My Current Read

Every canonical entity page should have Compiled Truth and Timeline from the first version.

## Counterexamples / Risks

Claims without source refs should be marked low-confidence or left as open questions.
